Start your new year with a great adventure with KrisFlyer Spontaneous Escapes redemption specials this January!

 

With more than 70 destinations on offer across the Singapore Airlines and SilkAir network, it’s the perfect opportunity to tick off your 2018 travel resolution.

 

Enjoy 30% KrisFlyer miles redemption discount when you redeem your KrisFlyer miles online for selected Singapore Airlines or SilkAir flights on singaporeair.com, for redemptions made from now till 31 December 2017.

 

In addition, receive an exclusive 40% KrisFlyer redemption discount for one-way flights departing selected Australian cities on Singapore Airlines or SilkAir this month.

 

Choose from any of the flights below and travel from 1 to 31 January 2018 inclusive.
