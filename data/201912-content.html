KrisFlyer Spontaneous Escapes

 

 

This festive season, treat yourself and your loved ones to the perfect year-end getaway. From a beach holiday to a winter wonderland, let your miles take you further with KrisFlyer Spontaneous Escapes’ 30% redemption discounts to destinations in the Singapore Airlines and SilkAir network!

 

Simply redeem your flights on singaporeair.com from now till 30 November 2019 to qualify, for travel from 1 to 31 December 2019 inclusive.

 

In addition, enjoy 30% off Premium Economy Class Saver rates on our non-stop flights between Singapore and Los Angeles or San Francisco, for travel from 1 to 31 January 2020 inclusive.

 

Seat availability is on a first-come, first-served basis, so hurry and redeem your flights now. Terms and conditions and blackout dates apply.

 

 

Travel Destinations

 

The number of KrisFlyer miles required for redemption shown below is for per person per one-way travel, after applying the discount. Taxes and fees will apply in addition to the required redemption mileage and must be paid separately by the passenger in the applicable currency.

 

Redemption discount applies strictly to flights listed in the table below. For return flight bookings, flight numbers for both sectors have to match with flights listed for the respective discounts to be valid. Blackout dates apply.

How to Redeem

 

To redeem, please complete the following:

  1. Log in to your KrisFlyer account with your KrisFlyer membership number and password at the top right corner of this page.
  2. Select ‘Book a Flight’ on your dashboard.
  3. Click ‘Redeem flights’ and select your travel details.
  4. Redemption discount will only be reflected in the booking summary panel after you have selected the flights.

 

Seats are limited, and redemption availability is on a first-come-first-served basis.
